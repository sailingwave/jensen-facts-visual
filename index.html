The Jensen Huang Affair: Timeline and Source Tracing

From The New York Times' first report on 2026-10-29 to the board's acceptance of the resignation on 2026-11-11. Verification date 2026-11-15; all dates are US Eastern Time; only the final conclusions are stated. Click any node in the graphics for details and sources.

Conclusions

1. The first report was The New York Times on 2026-10-29, bylined by six reporters, with all sources anonymous.

2. The intelligence itself had existed for at least a year (Trump had been briefed about a year earlier, and the spring 2026 memo had already circulated); 10-29 was the decision to publish, not the arrival of new information.

3. The escalation came from the subsequent drip: within hours of the 11-11 evening reports — the Powell second-affair story and the WSJ report that the board was preparing to remove Huang — the board accepted his resignation; the 8-K gave no reason, and the findings of the investigation were not published. Sequence in time is not causation.

4. Congressional action followed the reporting: the Nov 4 letter explicitly cited "recent reports" as its reason, with no footnotes and no citations; the footnotes of the Dec 3 hearing request cite NYT and WSJ reporting directly.

5. The source of the leak cannot be confirmed: the public record cannot establish where the information came from.

Source of the leak: cannot be confirmed

The public record cannot confirm where the information came from. The reasons are as follows:

  1. Being first only proves publication order: that NYT published first on Oct 29 only proves it published the story first; it does not prove who its anonymous sources were.
  2. The first story's description of its sources points to knowledgeable people inside the government ("people briefed on the surveillance"), not to any outside organization — but that only describes the type of source, not an identity; no material examined names the leaker.
  3. Matching wording is likewise not attribution evidence: the similar phrasing in later reporting is mostly same-source repetition (the same company statement, the same 8-K, secondhand descriptions of the same memo) or single-source republication — not independent verification by multiple outlets, and still less evidence of coordinated action by multiple outlets.

What would still be needed to confirm the source of the leak:

  • A source chain for a named source or a leaked document (who gave the memo to which outlet);
  • Records of communication between the reporting outlets and any interested party;
  • Internal records of the board's decision (what changed the board's mind on the evening of 11-11).

None of the above currently exists in the public record. Until then, the question of "where the information came from" has no timeline-based answer, only position-based speculation. As of the public record through 2026-11-13, any claim that the source of the leak has already been confirmed has no direct evidence behind it.

从《纽约时报》2026-10-29 首发，到董事会 2026-11-11 接受辞职。核查日期 2026-11-15；日期一律美东时间；只讲最终结论。点击图中任意节点查看详情与出处。

结论

1. 首发是《纽约时报》2026-10-29，六人署名，信源全匿名。

2. 情报本身存在至少一年（特朗普约一年前已知情、2026 年春备忘录已传阅），10-29 是决定公开，不是信息新生。

3. 升级靠后续 drip：11-11 晚 Powell 第二段婚外情报道与 WSJ 罢免报道发出后数小时，董事会接受了辞职；8-K 未给理由，调查结论未公布。时间上的先后不等于因果。

4. 国会动作跟在报道之后：11 月 4 日信件明确以“近期报道”为理由，信中无脚注、无引用；12 月 3 日听证请求的脚注直接引用 NYT 与 WSJ 的报道。

5. 泄密来源无法确认：公开记录无法确认消息来自哪里。

泄密来源：无法确认

公开记录无法确认消息来自哪里。理由如下：

  1. 先发只证明发表顺序：NYT 10 月 29 日首发，只能证明它先发表了报道，不能证明它的匿名信源是谁。
  2. 首发篇的信源描述指向政府内部知情人（“people briefed on the surveillance”），而非任何外部组织——但这只是信源类型的描述，不是身份；没有任何已查材料点名泄露者。
  3. 口径一致同样不是归属证据：后续报道的雷同措辞基本是同源复述（同一份公司声明、同一份 8-K、同一份备忘录的二手描述）或单源转载，不是多家独立核实，更不是多家协同行动的证据。

要确认泄密来源，还缺什么：

  • 具名信源或泄密文件的来源链（谁把备忘录给了哪家媒体）；
  • 报道机构与任何利益方之间的沟通记录；
  • 董事会决策的内部记录（是什么让董事会在 11 月 11 日晚改变主意）。

上述任何一项在公开记录中目前都不存在。在此之前，“消息来自哪里”这个问题没有基于时间线的答案，只有基于立场的猜测。截至 2026-11-13 的公开记录，任何声称已经确认泄密来源的说法，都没有直接证据支撑。
