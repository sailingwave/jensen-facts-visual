✓ Inflows and outflows traceable — AnthroPAC

A traditional PAC, with all FEC inflows and outflows public. By law the company may not fund it — only employees may give (up to $5,000 per person per year) — which is why the total is only just over $400,000.

Notes

  1. Line widths scale with the square root of the amount (not linearly); figures follow the labels and the flow table. Formula: width = 0.004427 × √amount (USD); $40M caps at 28.0px and $300k floors at 2.4px. A linear scale is unworkable for this data ($300k would be only 0.21px).
  2. Attribution break: the break line is drawn only at PFA's exits, cutting only money leaving PFA (→ J&D, → DOV, → PUBLIC FIRST, → the ad budget); outflows from other sources such as PUBLIC FIRST and AnthroPAC do not pass through the break. PFA is a 501(c)(4) and does not disclose donors; the $40M is mixed with other sources in the pool, so no particular dollar can be shown to have gone to any particular super PAC. Anthropic's “not for use in elections” restriction is a unilateral company statement; the public record can neither confirm it was honoured nor confirm it was violated.
  3. Second-order caveat: money entering a super PAC's pool and then leaving it (PUBLIC FIRST → J&D $4,040,000, → DOV $300,000) breaks attribution a second time — there is no way to say how much of it was Amodei's and how much was PFA's.
  4. The $15M for ads is a project budget, not a transfer (grey dashed line; line width does not represent the amount); PFA's self-reported network-wide fundraising of about $80M (2026-06-30) cannot be independently verified and is not shown as a node.
  5. What was left out of this diagram, and why: RAND, 17 transactions / $86.956M, and IFP, 3 transactions / $21.457M — no primary grant list found; “PFA→Public First, 9 transactions, $8.5M” — no such combination appears in FEC records (that super PAC's total receipts for the full cycle are only $4,910,605); the Moskovitzes' “$4.4B to Coefficient Giving in 2021–2025” — Forbes in fact reports cumulative giving of >$4 billion since 2011, across all areas; AnthroPAC's “$195,000 given through November” — FEC records show $320,750 already given through October 14.
  6. Scope: employee personal gifts are counted for the four large donors at $2,149,900; with Jermyn's $5,000, the five total $2,154,900, or 64.2% of PUBLIC FIRST's total receipts. AnthroPAC's $403,937 is total receipts, not a single transfer. PFA's total transfers to the three affiliated super PACs were $24,284,052.67.
  7. Share basis: the denominator of “share of recipient's total receipts” is the recipient committee's FEC 2026-cycle total receipts (fed_receipts, committee totals through 2026-09-30): Jobs and Democracy PAC $20,735,723.37, Defending Our Values PAC $8,406,067.63, PUBLIC FIRST $4,910,605. J&D's remaining $1,296,738 (6.3%) and DOV's remaining $221,000 (2.6%) came from other sources. The ad project is a budget with no total-receipts denominator; AnthroPAC direct contributions went to multiple candidate committees and party committees, so no share was calculated — 79.4% is the ratio of its total direct contributions of $320,750 to AnthroPAC's own total receipts.
  8. Column 4 basis (do not mix the two mechanisms): by law a super PAC may not donate directly to a candidate and can only make independent expenditures — the blue lines read “independent expenditure supporting/opposing X”, meaning the PAC itself spent money to take a position, not that it gave money to X; AnthroPAC is a traditional PAC, and the orange lines read “direct contribution to X”, money paid straight into the campaign committee ($5,000 limit per person per election). The attribution basis is unchanged: once PFA's money enters a super PAC's pool it is mixed together, so one can only say “that PAC spent $Y supporting X”, not “Anthropic's money went to X”.
  9. Reconciliation for the three PACs (do not pretend receipts equal spending): J&D receipts $20,735,723.37, spending $20,065,333.68 (independent expenditures $17,875,336.49 + operations $2,189,997.19), closing cash $670,389.69; DOV receipts $8,406,067.63, spending $7,773,196.83 (independent expenditures $7,365,832.10 + operations $407,364.73), closing cash $632,870.80; AnthroPAC receipts $403,936.66, spending $321,669.11 (line 23 contributions $320,750.00 + operations $919.11), closing cash $82,267.55. Each gap exactly equals closing cash.   13. Small lines use the minimum width: for AnthroPAC's lines ($5,000 / $3,500 / $125,000 / $159,250) the square-root formula gives widths below the 2.4px floor, so all are drawn at 2.4px; amounts follow the node labels and the candidate table.
