# jensen-facts-visual

Fact-checked visualizations about the November 2026 resignation of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, and the Anthropic-related political money flows behind the public debate.

- [Timeline: from the first report to the resignation](index.html) — 30 events, October 29 – November 11, 2026, each with its sources, plus a 15-item appendix for November 12–13.
- [Anthropic-related money flows](money-flow.html) — flows verified against FEC filings (super PACs through September 30, 2026; AnthroPAC through October 14, 2026), including who the PACs' spending supported.

Both pages are single self-contained HTML files with no build step. Each item links to its sources. Text is English by default with a Chinese toggle in the top-right corner.
